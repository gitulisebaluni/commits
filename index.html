Who we are / 01

Local ambition.
Real-world skills.

GITULISE is the innovation and technology department of Zombo District PHAS Network Forum.

Our mission is to uplift young people in our villages by equipping them with practical life-support and digital skills. Through software development, general ICT training and an emphasis on economic empowerment, we help emerging talent become a valuable force in their communities.

● Community first. Future focused.
