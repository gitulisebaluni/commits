Awadifor is an offline arithmetic puzzle game developed by GITULISE. No account. No sign-in. No ads or analytics. No personal data is collected or sent to us through the game.
01 / THE BASICS

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Awadifor (the “App”), developed and published by GITULISE (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use it. Awadifor offers multi-level, grid-based number and arithmetic puzzles designed for users aged 13 and above.

The App is intended to function entirely on your device, without requiring an internet connection or a user account.

02 / YOUR INFORMATION

Information we collect

Awadifor does not collect personal information through the App. We do not request your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal details to play.

We do not use in-app analytics, advertising trackers, or behavioral profiling. We do not collect gameplay information on our servers.

No account required

You can start playing without registering, signing in, or providing any personal information.

03 / ON YOUR DEVICE

Local game preferences

The App may save a small amount of non-personal game information on your device, such as your most recently selected difficulty level, the number of puzzles completed, and whether you have viewed the introductory tutorial.

These preferences are stored locally using the device's application storage. They are not transmitted to GITULISE or any external server. You can remove them by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

04 / OFFLINE BY DESIGN

Permissions and connectivity

Awadifor does not require an internet connection for gameplay. The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or device files for its puzzle features.

The App does not present runtime permission requests as part of normal gameplay. Your operating system may still provide standard app installation and system controls.

05 / NO DATA SHARING

Third parties and advertising

Awadifor does not contain third-party advertising, in-app analytics SDKs, social sign-in, or cloud synchronization. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data collected through the App, because the App does not collect such data.

If you download Awadifor through Google Play, Google may independently process information related to your download, installation, payments (if applicable), or use of Google Play under Google's own privacy policies. That processing is separate from Awadifor's in-app behavior.

06 / AGE SUITABILITY

Children's privacy

Awadifor is designed for individuals aged 13 and above. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. It does not offer chat, social networking, user profiles, or other user-to-user communication features.

Parents and guardians may review this policy and use device-level controls where appropriate. Any age rating or target-audience declarations in Google Play should reflect the App's actual content and intended users.

07 / YOUR CONTROL

Security, retention and deletion

Because Awadifor does not transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a remote database of players' personal information. The limited preferences described above remain on your device until cleared, removed by uninstalling the App, or affected by device-level backup or restore features.

To erase the App's locally saved preferences, use your device's app settings to clear its storage or uninstall the App. There is no online account to delete.

08 / STAY INFORMED

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Awadifor's functionality, applicable legal requirements, or privacy practices change. When we do, we will update the effective date and publish the revised policy at the same publicly accessible location.

If a future App version introduces features that change data handling, we will update this policy and any applicable Google Play Data safety disclosures accordingly.
